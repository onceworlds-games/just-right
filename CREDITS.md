# Credits

Every model in `public/models` is CC0 1.0 (public domain), downloaded from poly.pizza, where each model page names its author and
license. `tools/assets.mjs` merges them into `cast.glb`, `props.glb` and `nature.glb` (shrunk textures, meshopt-compressed). The
portraits in `public/faces` and the storybook pictures in `public/pages` are rendered from the game itself.
